# ACME GmbH – Employee Handbook (sample)

## Vacation

Full-time employees get 30 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation requests
are approved by your team lead in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over until 31 March of the following year.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Core hours are
10:00–15:00. Working from abroad needs approval from HR for tax reasons.

## Expenses

Travel expenses are reimbursed within 14 days after submitting receipts.
Train travel is second class; first class is allowed for trips over 4 hours.
Questions go to finance@acme.example or +49 69 1234 5678.

## IT security

All laptops use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported
to the IT helpdesk immediately. Customer data must never be pasted into public
AI tools; use the internal assistant instead.
